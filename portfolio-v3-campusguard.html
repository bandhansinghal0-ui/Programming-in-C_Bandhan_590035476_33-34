Featured C Mini Project · UPES Bidholi

CampusGuard

Smart Emergency Navigation & Response System. CampusGuard is a student-built C prototype for emergency navigation, response and preparedness at the UPES Bidholi campus. It models a campus road network and dynamically compares available exits to recommend a route while accounting for reported hazards, road problems, exit availability and accessibility requirements.
